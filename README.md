# Awesome-Manufacturing-Knowledge-Platform

## Top Manufacturing Knowledge Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Digital Work Instructions, Connected Worker, Standard Work, Skills, Tribal Knowledge Capture & Frontline Guidance*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Manufacturing Knowledge Platforms** (connected worker / digital work instructions). These systems capture expert knowledge, deliver step-by-step guidance on the shop floor, manage skills and training, and help manufacturers standardize work across shifts and sites.



**Examples** include Poka, Dozuki, SwipeGuide, Operations1, Tulip, Augmentir, REWO, KnowledgeVine, Azumuta, and Intoware (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full connected-worker platforms are mostly commercial. Practical open options include **digital work-instruction prototypes**, **AR guidance experiments**, knowledge-graph frameworks, and general wiki/SOP tools adapted for manufacturing. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Poka](https://www.poka.io/)**  

  Connected worker platform combining digital work instructions, skills management, checklists, and frontline engagement for manufacturers.



- **[Dozuki](https://www.dozuki.com/)**  

  AI-powered connected worker platform focused on digitizing standard work, version control, training, and knowledge sharing—strong in regulated environments.



- **[SwipeGuide](https://www.swipeguide.com/)**  

  Digital work instructions and connected worker platform emphasizing visual, mobile-friendly guidance and global rollouts.



- **[Operations1](https://operations1.com/)**  

  Connected worker and digital work instruction platform for structured shop-floor processes, quality, and knowledge management.



- **[Tulip](https://tulip.co/)**  

  No-code frontline operations platform that lets teams build apps for work instructions, data capture, and machine connectivity without traditional coding.



- **[Augmentir](https://www.augmentir.com/)**  

  AI-powered connected worker platform delivering personalized work instructions, skills insights, and remote support for industrial frontline teams.



- **[REWO](https://www.rewo.io/)**  

  Digital work instruction and knowledge-capture platform focused on visual standard work and shop-floor guidance.



- **[KnowledgeVine](https://www.knowledgevine.com/)**  

  Manufacturing knowledge and work instruction solutions aimed at capturing and distributing operational expertise.



- **[Azumuta](https://www.azumuta.com/)**  

  Connected worker platform offering visual digital work instructions, skills matrices, and integration with shop-floor systems.



- **[Intoware](https://www.intoware.com/)**  

  Digital work instructions and connected worker technology supporting guided procedures and frontline knowledge delivery.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[AR Work Instructions open prototypes](https://github.com/)**  

  Emerging open-source projects that render step-by-step assembly, maintenance, and safety procedures in augmented reality on the shop floor.



- **[Human-centric knowledge graph frameworks (e.g. HCKG-style)](https://github.com/)**  

  Research and open frameworks that model operator work, ergonomics, and collaboration using knowledge graphs for Industry 5.0 scenarios.



- **[Wiki and SOP open platforms](https://github.com/)**  

  Self-hosted wiki and documentation systems (Wiki.js, BookStack, Outline, etc.) adapted for controlled standard operating procedures and work instructions.



- **[Digital checklist and form open tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Open form and checklist builders used to create step-by-step procedures and capture shop-floor execution data.



- **[Odoo / ERPNext manufacturing and quality docs](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  Open ERP modules that support work centers, routing, quality checks, and document links usable as a lightweight knowledge layer.



- **[Media-rich procedure open authoring kits](https://github.com/)**  

  Templates and small apps for authoring photo/video-based work instructions exported to the floor.



- **[Skills matrix and training open trackers](https://github.com/)**  

  Simple open tools for tracking operator skills, certifications, and training completion.



- **[QR / asset-linked instruction open servers](https://github.com/)**  

  Lightweight services that serve work instructions when operators scan equipment QR codes.



- **[Offline-first progressive web apps for the floor](https://github.com/)**  

  Open PWA patterns designed for reliable access to procedures in low-connectivity plant environments.



- **[Translation and multi-language open pipelines](https://github.com/)**  

  Tools that help maintain multi-language versions of standard work for global plants.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Building standard work in **open wikis or document systems** with strict version control and approval workflows.

- Experimenting with **open AR work-instruction prototypes** for high-value assembly or maintenance tasks.

- Using **Odoo/ERPNext** document and quality modules as a starting knowledge layer tied to production orders.

- Accepting that polished connected-worker experiences, AI-assisted authoring, skills analytics, and multi-site governance still favor commercial platforms (Poka, Dozuki, Tulip, SwipeGuide, Augmentir, Azumuta, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership, cost control for smaller plants, and capturing tribal knowledge without lock-in.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Author procedures in an open wiki or form tool → link to assets via QR → deliver on tablets or wearables → capture completion and feedback → improve content in closed-loop reviews. Suitable for plants with strong continuous-improvement culture and IT support. Many manufacturers still adopt commercial connected-worker platforms for speed, usability, and scale.
